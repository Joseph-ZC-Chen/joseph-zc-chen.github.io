## Research Projects:
- A Configurable Fault-Tolerant Automated Testing Framework for Large-Scale Payment Systems<br/>
Sep 2025 – Jan 2026 | Project Lead & Core Designer<br/>Problem Statement<br/> Conventional end-to-end payment testing in banking relies on manual transaction initiation. In production-like scenario testing, this creates three critical bottlenecks: limited manual throughput, mandatory full re-runs after batch tool failures, and high cross-subsystem verification costs — all failing to meet batch validation requirements for ISO 20022 migration.<br/>Methodology<br/>Proposed and implemented a three-tier automated testing framework:<br/>• **Multi-dimensional parametric scheduler**: flexible configuration of transaction volume, transmission rate, and target nodes<br/>• **Log-aware fault-tolerant resumption**: non-intrusive runtime state tracking with automatic breakpoint recovery, no full re-execution required<br/>• **Hierarchical verification engine**: three-level automated validation covering execution status, business logic consistency, and anomaly severity grading<br/>Results<br/>Supports fully automated submission and verification of 10,000+ payment transactions per batch<br/>Reduces abnormal interruption recovery time by over 90%<br/>Cuts end-to-end testing labor input by 70%<br/>Deployed in a national banking regulatory compliance testing program<br/><br/>

- BA Agent: An LLM-Powered Assistant for Software Requirements Analysis and User Story Generation<br/>
Mar 2026 – Present | Project Lead<br/>Problem Statement<br/> Requirements engineering for banking software is highly dependent on the domain expertise of senior business analysts (BAs), resulting in inconsistent output quality from junior staff and low efficiency in manual end-to-end translation from business requirements to development artifacts and test cases.<br/>
Methodology<br/>Designed and developed a domain-specific intelligent requirements analysis agent for banking software scenarios. Built on VS Code with GitHub Copilot Chat integration, the agent encodes institutional banking payment knowledge and process standards, and automatically transforms natural-language business requirements into standardized, enterprise-compliant user stories.<br/>
Results<br/>Generated user stories fully align with internal enterprise BA governance and specification standards<br/>Delivers material efficiency gains in the conversion from requirement documents to development deliverables<br/>Ongoing extension to full-lifecycle coverage, including automated test case generation and automated defect triage<br/><br/>
